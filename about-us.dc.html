About Greenway Links

A Partner in Your Supply Chain.

Connecting people, materials and movement is how we add value.

Who we are

Greenway Links Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian logistics and operational support company based in Shah Alam, Selangor. Serving primarily Klang Valley and Port Klang, we provide integrated logistics and third-party logistics (3PL) services spanning industrial transport, warehousing, manufacturing support, workforce solutions and fulfilment.

What connects our services

Our strengths in industrial logistics and manufacturing operational support shape how we approach customer requirements. We understand that transport schedules, material availability, on-site workforce and warehouse operations are closely linked. Alongside these core services, we provide container haulage, warehousing, manpower and fulfilment solutions that can be coordinated to suit each operation.

Our approach

We focus on understanding the task before recommending the service. Whether that means moving containers from port to manufacturing plant, supporting a production team or arranging fulfilment for multiple sales channels, we aim to provide a clear, workable solution.
