Who We Are

Greenway Links Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian logistics and operational support company based in Shah Alam, Selangor. Serving primarily Klang Valley and Port Klang, we provide integrated logistics and third-party logistics (3PL) services spanning industrial transport, warehousing, manufacturing support, workforce solutions and fulfilment.
