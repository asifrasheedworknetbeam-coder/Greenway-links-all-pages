Fulfilment Solutions

B2B, DTC and marketplace fulfilment. Designed to support accurate order processing, efficient inventory management, and timely dispatch across your online sales channels.

Overview

Greenway Links provides fulfilment solutions tailored to the needs of online & retail businesses, brands, and online marketplace sellers. From inventory receiving and storage to order processing, picking, packing, dispatch, and returns management, we manage your day-to-day fulfilment operations with a focus on accuracy, efficiency, and reliability, allowing you to focus on growing your business.

01

B2B & Wholesale Fulfilment

Tailored fulfilment solutions for brands, wholesalers, distributors, and suppliers serving business and retail customers. Our services cover bulk order processing, carton and pallet picking, packing, labelling, and dispatch coordination to support efficient wholesale and distribution operations.

02

E-commerce & DTC Fulfilment

Fulfilment solutions for brands and online businesses selling directly to consumers through websites and digital storefronts. Our services cover storage, inventory management, individual order processing, picking, packing, and dispatch, with customised packaging options to support your brand's presentation and customer experience.

03

Marketplace Fulfilment

For sellers managing orders through marketplaces such as Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop. We support storing, picking, packing, labelling and dispatch workflows appropriate to the chosen sales channels and fulfilment arrangements.

Selling through more than one channel?

A business may supply retailers in bulk, sell through its own online shop and serve marketplace orders at the same time. We can coordinate workflows across these channels using a shared warehouse team and inventory processes designed around your order mix.
